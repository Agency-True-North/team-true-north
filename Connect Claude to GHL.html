01 / What This Gives You

Right now Claude knows your voice and your ideal client. After this, it can also see your actual business: who signed up, who booked, who went quiet, and what is sitting in your pipeline.

You stop exporting lists and pasting them into chat. You ask a question and Claude goes and looks. You ask for a follow-up email and Claude drafts it in your voice and loads it into GHL as a template, ready to use. You ask who has not been touched in two weeks and you get names.

Under the hood, this installs a small connector on your computer that Claude talks to. It is the same connector Shannon runs, with the broken parts of the public version already fixed, so Claude does not waste your afternoon chasing errors that are not yours.

02 / Before You Start

Three things, then you are ready.

1

Your Claude workspace is set up

You finished the Claude Setup Guide, so you have a Claude folder open in VS Code with a CLAUDE.md file in it. If you have not, do that guide first. This one builds on it.

2

You can log in to your own GHL sub-account

The sub-account is the view that shows your contacts and conversations, not the agency dashboard. Have it open in a browser tab before you paste the box.

3

Fifteen uninterrupted minutes

Claude walks you through the GHL part one click at a time and waits for you. Starting in the school pickup line works. Starting between two Zoom calls does not.

Read the file .claude/skills/connect-ghl/SKILL.md and follow it exactly, step by step, starting now. The rules at the top of that file apply to this whole conversation.
Claude will ask your permission as it works

When Claude downloads a file or runs a command, a small prompt appears asking you to allow it. Click Yes or Allow. That is the approval system doing its job. You are always in charge of what Claude does on your computer.

Closed the laptop halfway through?

Paste the same box again in a new conversation. Claude checks what is already done and picks up at the next step. Nothing repeats and nothing is lost.

04 / What Claude Will Ask You For

Two things live inside GHL that only you can reach. Claude cues each one when it is time, one instruction per message. This section is here so none of it surprises you.

1

Your Location ID (not secret)

While you are inside your sub-account, Claude asks you to copy the web address from your browser's address bar and paste it into the chat. Your account number is hiding inside that address. Claude pulls it out and confirms the last few characters back to you.

Where it isThe chunk of letters and numbers right after /location/ in the address. Claude finds it; you only copy the address.
2

A Private Integration token (secret)

This is the key that gives Claude permission to work in your account. You create it inside GHL, you own it, and you can delete it any time from the same screen.

The clicks Claude will walk you throughSettings (bottom of the left menu) → Private Integrations → Create new Integration → name it Claude → tick every permission in the list → Create → Copy. GHL shows the token one time only.
The token goes into a file, never into the chat

Claude has already made a file in your Claude folder called .mcp.json with a blank spot that says PASTE_YOUR_PRIVATE_INTEGRATION_TOKEN_HERE. You click that file in VS Code, paste your token over those words (keep the quote marks on both sides), and save. Claude checks the blank is filled without ever reading the token out loud. Chat history travels; that file stays on your computer and is kept out of any backups.

Don't see Private Integrations in Settings?

Look for Labs in the same Settings menu and turn Private Integrations on there. If there is no Labs either, your agency has it switched off. Message Shannon with the words "please enable Private Integrations on my sub-account," then paste the box again when she confirms. Claude resumes right at this step.

Tick every permission. Here is why.

GHL calls them scopes, and there are dozens. Every one you skip is a future afternoon where Claude says it cannot see your calendar. Ticking all of them now costs nothing. The key only works from your computer, and you can revoke it in two clicks if you ever want to.

05 / The Restart

Before the restart, Claude runs a check that proves your key opens your account and names the sub-account it connected to. Only after that check passes does it ask you to do this:

1

Reload the window

Press Cmd+Shift+P on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+P on Windows, type Reload Window, press Enter. VS Code blinks and comes back.

2

Approve the connector

Open the Claude panel. A small prompt may say a new MCP server named ghl was found in this project. Choose the option that uses it.

3

Paste the box once more

Start a new conversation and paste the Section 03 box again. Claude sees the setup is finished, runs the live test, and tells you in plain words what it can now see in your account.

06 / What to Ask Claude Now

Talk to it like a teammate who has your GHL open on the other screen. A few starters:

This week's signupsWho signed up in my GHL this week? Give me names, where they came from, and whether they've booked anything.
One person, full pictureFind the contact named [name] and show me our last conversation, their tags, and where they sit in my pipeline.
Pipeline checkWhat's sitting in my pipeline with no activity in the last 14 days? Suggest a next step for each one.
Draft into GHLDraft a follow-up email in my voice for people who booked a call and didn't show. Show it to me first, then load it into GHL as an email template.
The one rule Claude keeps

Reading and drafting are always safe to ask for. Claude never sends a message to a real contact, deletes anything, or changes a live automation unless you say yes to that exact action in that conversation.

07 / What Claude Can't Do in GHL

GHL does not open these doors to any outside tool, so no connector can do them. Knowing this up front saves you from waiting on something that will not happen.

You wantWhat happensDo this instead
Build or edit a funnel pageNot possible from outside GHLClaude writes the copy and the page spec. You build it in the GHL page editor.
Create or edit a workflowClaude can list workflows and add people to them, nothing moreClaude plans the steps and writes every email. You click them into the workflow. Click Save on the action and Save in the top right, every time.
Send an email campaignSending is blocked for outside toolsClaude creates the email as a template in GHL. You load it into a campaign and send.
Manage courses or membershipsNo outside access at allStays in the GHL screens.

08 / Troubleshooting

Claude has this same table and will work through it with you. Check here before assuming anything is broken.

What you seeWhat's causing itWhat to do
Claude says it has no GHL tools after the restartWindow not reloaded, or the connector prompt was dismissedReload Window again. When the prompt about the ghl server appears, choose to use it.
The connection times out the first timeThe first start can be slow on some computersTell Claude "run the preflight again," then Reload Window. The second start is fast.
Every request fails with 401Token copied wrong, or created at the agency levelCreate a fresh Private Integration inside the sub-account and paste it into the file again.
One kind of request fails with 403That permission was not tickedSettings → Private Integrations → Claude → edit the scopes → tick everything. No new token needed.
Claude sees someone else's contactsLocation ID copied from the wrong sub-accountTell Claude "my Location ID is wrong." It redoes that step with you.
You pasted the token into the chatHabitClaude moves it into the file and carries on. Next time, the file.
The download step failsA hiccup reaching the team siteTell Claude: "Try the downloads again, and if they still fail, use raw.githubusercontent.com/Agency-True-North/team-true-north/main/ instead of the team site address."
Still stuck after two tries

Send Shannon a screenshot of what Claude last said and what your GHL screen shows. One message with both beats five messages with neither.
